# Winning Ways

A Balatro mod about Conway's numbers and games, named for Berlekamp, Conway and Guy's *Winning Ways for your
Mathematical Plays*. Cards become surreal numbers, hot games, nimbers and ω: Cut two cards into {L | R}, Sum
them, heat and cool them, and let the Blind take its share of the hot ones.

**Status: design.** Nothing is implemented yet. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) is the sketch.

- **Real-valued ranks:** UnStable (kirbio) first did these in Balatro. Winning Ways reimplements the idea; it uses
