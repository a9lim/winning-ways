# Notice: art derived from Balatro

Winning Ways is MIT-licensed, but two of the built textures in this folder
are derived from Balatro's own sprites and contain pixels that are
(c) LocalThunk. They are **not** covered by the MIT licence:

- `1x/ranks.png`, `2x/ranks.png`
- `1x/ranks_hc.png`, `2x/ranks_hc.png`

What in them is Balatro's: the suit pips, the strokes of the "0" and "1"
index digits (from vanilla's "10"), the bodies of the four Cavaliers (the
vanilla Jacks, re-inked), and the high-contrast shading. What is ours: the
"C" index, the outline pip of the 0, the Cavaliers' helms and plumes, and
the composition.

The other textures contain no pixels from the game and are MIT like the
code:

- `1x/mods.png`, `2x/mods.png`, `1x/mods_hc.png`, `2x/mods_hc.png`
- `1x/glyphs.png`, `2x/glyphs.png`

They do use Balatro's interface colours.

The 0 as a hollow pip and the 1 as a single small pip are solutions shared
with UnStable (kirbio, MIT); no UnStable pixels are used.

Provenance in full: `art/NOTES.md`. The sprites are rebuilt from an
installed copy of the game by `art/build.py`; nothing from the game archive
is stored in this repository as source.
